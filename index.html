Four Core Empowerment Pillars

Structured Pathways From Potential to Scale

₦500K – ₦5M Tranches

Enterprise & Seed Capital

Non-dilutive micro-grants, equipment co-financing, and structured business mentorship for young women launching or scaling agro-processing, fashion, retail, and service enterprises.

Eligibility: Nigerian women aged 18–35 with verified CAC registration or cooperative endorsement and a 12-month cashflow plan.

Milestone Controls: Tranche disbursement tied to supplier invoices, bookkeeping clinics, and quarterly impact audits.

16-Week Intensive

Digital Commerce & AI Workflows

Practical training in e-commerce storefronts, payment gateways, data literacy, product photography, and applied AI workflows that expand reach beyond local street markets.

Curriculum: Digital merchandising, social & export logistics, financial record automation, and practical AI productivity.

Outcome: Capstone launch of a live digital storefront or tech-enabled service portfolio with mentor review.

Executive & Policy Track

Civic, Policy & Executive Leadership

Leadership fellowships preparing young Nigerian women for board participation, public policy advocacy, community governance, and executive decision-making roles.

Modules: Public policy analysis, boardroom governance, strategic communications, and coalition building.

Placement: Policy briefs, town-hall roundtables, and executive shadowing across partner institutions.

Dignity & Protection

Health Access, Legal Aid & Safety

Wrap-around support connecting young women with preventive health screenings, legal literacy, contract & IP protection for founders, and safe community spaces.

Safeguards: Confidential legal clinics, business registration guidance, and mental & maternal wellness referrals.

Standard: Zero-tolerance safeguarding protocols across every YWFEI hub, camp, and training venue.
